How to play

Survive until the alarm rings at 6 AM.

On a touch screen: drag across the room to look around, tap DOOR and LIGHT on each side, tap CAMERAS at the bottom, and tap ☾ to pause.

Look around: move the mouse to the screen edges, or use ← →.

Left side: Q door, A light. Right side: E door, D light.

SPACE cameras, 1–9 switch camera, F pinch yourself, ESC pause.

Everything drains Lucidity. If it hits 0%, the dream stops listening to you.

A shut door is like holding your breath: cheap for a little while, then it costs more and more. Open it once it bangs.

Pinching yourself gives Lucidity back, more the lower you are. But it is loud, and it draws the monster closer. Never pinch while it breathes at an open door: it comes straight in.

You’ll hear it before you see it: it clicks in the walls, louder the closer it gets. It always breathes at your door before it comes in.

Below the line on the Lucidity bar, the dream starts to lie. A fake never moves, glows violet, and its breathing runs backwards. The light shows the truth, and a pinch clears every lie.

The dream voice speaks a little at a time: click or tap it to hurry it on. The pause menu remembers what it said.

At 6 AM the alarm rings and you wake up in your room. Turn it off, then look around: sometimes something follows you out.
