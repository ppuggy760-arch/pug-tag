## Pictures in the rooms (the built-in photo stand-ins)

Until you draw your own rooms into `assets/rooms/`, the game shows its own rooms from
`assets/scenes/`. Each one was put together from many free pictures from the internet:
photos of old wood, plaster, wallpaper, velvet and carpets, old photographs of real old
houses, old paintings, museum photos of old toys, 3-D scans of real old furniture and clocks,
and NASA's moon. They were colour-matched and lit so they all look like one house (the recipe
is in `art-src/compose/`; it rebuilds every picture). None of them shows a monster.

Every source below is **CC0** or **public domain**, so no attribution is required. They are
credited anyway, as thanks. (No CC BY, CC BY-SA or non-commercial pictures are used.)
The crayon drawing pinned in the desk room is drawn by the game itself, on the old paper below.

* Textures from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org): CC0 1.0.
* Wallpapers, carpets and quilts photographed by The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Rijksmuseum and the
  Cooper Hewitt (Smithsonian Design Museum): CC0 (open access) or public domain (old 2D works, PD-Art / PD-old).
* Old paintings (all painted more than 100 years ago): public domain.
* Old photographs of real houses from the Historic American Buildings Survey (Library of Congress): public domain (US government work).
* The moon: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University, public domain (PD-NASA). (On Wikimedia Commons the full-moon
  photo is also tagged CC BY 2.0 from its Flickr upload; it is used here under its PD-NASA tag.)
* 3-D scans (the armchairs, your chair, the alarm clock, the grandfather clock, the cot and the nursery's chest):
  [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) models, CC0 1.0. Their textures are photographs of the real objects.
* The rocking horse: Smithsonian American Art Museum open access, CC0.
