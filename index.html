THE GAME

INFECTION: one lava pug starts IT. Everyone it touches turns to lava. Survive the timer to win bones.
CLASSIC TAG: pass IT on with a touch. No tag-backs. Spend the least time as IT.
CASUAL: no tagging. Explore, climb and collect bones.

ITEMS

BONES: currency for cosmetics.
ZOOMIES (yellow bolt): faster pushes.
BUBBLE (blue): blocks one tag.
MOON PAWS (grey moon): low gravity.
TENNIS BALLS: throw one and nearby pugs, even lava ones, may run off to fetch it.

IN-WORLD

Every map has a PUG-OS terminal in its spawn kennel: change map, mode, bots and VR settings by pressing its buttons with your paws (or E / click). Bark Park's lodge also has the PET SHOP, a FUR COLOR board, a SCOREBOARD and a MIRROR.
